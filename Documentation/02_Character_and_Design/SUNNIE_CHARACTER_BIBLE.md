# Sunnie Character Bible

## 1. Role

Sunnie is the protagonist, guide, emotional center, and principal collectible character of Sunnie Days. He is not merely a logo or notification mascot. His design must remain recognizable across every outfit, pose, theme, destination, screen size, and future rendering method.

## 2. Canonical references

Use these files as the visual source of truth:

- `Reference_Images/Canonical/Sunnie_Canon_01_Character_Sheet.png`

The context references show desired environments and occupations, but not canonical age or proportions.

## 3. Core identity

- Species: sloth
- Gender: male
- Apparent age/style: young, baby-faced, plush-toy cute
- Personality: sleepy, warm, delighted, patient, curious, nurturing, gentle
- Emotional register: consistently positive; calm states are allowed, negativity is not
- Relationship to Vanessa: affectionate personal companion, never authority figure

## 4. Canonical proportions

Production art should use a formal model sheet based on these rules:

- Head is large relative to body.
- Face is broad and round, not long.
- Body is short and compact.
- Arms and legs are short and plush.
- Hands and feet are small with simplified rounded claws.
- Torso should never appear adult-human tall or broad-shouldered.
- Standing height should read as a small toy or childlike mascot.
- Seated poses should preserve a soft rounded silhouette.

Provisional ratio guidance:

- Head height: approximately 42–48 percent of total standing height
- Head width: slightly wider than torso
- Limb length: short enough that hands remain close to torso in relaxed poses
- Eye diameter: intentionally oversized relative to real sloth anatomy

These ratios are guidance for consistency, not mathematical requirements for every expressive pose.

## 5. Face

Required:

- Large glossy dark-brown eyes
- Warm highlights in the eyes
- Rounded cream face mask
- Soft darker eye patches
- Small dark nose
- Small smiling mouth
- Peach-pink cheek blush
- Short soft eyebrows
- Small hair tuft centered or slightly asymmetrical

Sleepy expressions may use half-lidded eyes, but the eyes, cheeks, and round face must preserve a youthful appearance.

Avoid:

- Deep eye bags
- Wrinkles
- Narrow eyes that make him appear elderly
- Long muzzle
- Human adult jawline
- Realistic teeth
- Angry brows

## 6. Fur and palette

Base colors are warm and soft:

- Main fur: light caramel/tan
- Face: creamy ivory
- Eye patches: medium warm brown
- Nose and claws: deep warm brown, not pure black when contrast allows
- Blush: soft peach-pink

Texture should feel plush, brushed, quilted, or softly rendered. Do not use hyper-real individual hair detail that pushes the design toward wildlife photography.

## 7. Default wardrobe

The default cozy look may include:

- Butter-yellow quilted pajamas or sweater
- Small sun emblem
- Soft nightcap when sleeping
- Lavender blanket or pillow
- Warm mug for calm scenes

Wardrobe is context-dependent. Sunnie may wear:

- Male flight-attendant-inspired uniform
- Gardening overalls
- Destination outfits
- Pajamas
- Travel clothing
- Seasonal clothing
- Wellness clothing

Every outfit must be resized and simplified for his canonical small body.

## 8. Flight-attendant version

The flight-attendant reference is a setting and costume guide only. Rebuild it with:

- Canonical young head and body proportions
- Navy tailored jacket simplified for a small plush body
- White shirt
- Red tie or neck detail
- Small name badge
- Optional private Delta-inspired accent assets
- Small roller bag
- Warm, competent, cheerful expression

Do not reproduce the tall adult body from the context image.

## 9. Garden version

The garden reference is an environment and action guide only. Rebuild it with:

- Canonical young body
- Small green overalls
- Soft shirt
- Gardening gloves sized for small paws
- Small watering can
- Tropical plants and flowers
- Joyful, gentle caretaking pose

## 10. Expression library

Minimum expression set for the 2D release:

- Happy open-eyed
- Happy closed-eyed
- Sleepy half-lidded
- Sleeping
- Gentle wave
- Proud
- Curious
- Calm breathing
- Excited discovery
- Hugging object
- Thinking
- Traveling
- Caring for plant
- Celebrating quietly
- Comforting/reassuring

No angry, disappointed, scolding, frightened, or distressed expression is required for the product.

## 11. Pose library

Minimum pose categories:

- Standing neutral
- Sitting neutral
- Waving
- Stretching
- Sleeping curled up
- Hugging pillow
- Holding mug
- Holding watering can
- Holding passport or postcard
- Pulling suitcase
- Reading
- Meditating
- Pointing to a card or task
- Wearing watch/travel uniform
- Decorating home
- Playing a game

## 12. Asset construction for current release

Prefer layered 2D assets so simple motion is possible without a full character rig:

- Body/base
- Face/eye variants
- Mouth variants
- Arm/hand overlays where necessary
- Outfit overlays
- Handheld prop overlays
- Optional foreground/background layers

Export production assets with transparent backgrounds at multiple scales. Keep source art in a non-runtime creator directory and optimized runtime assets in the asset catalog.

## 13. Character behavior

Sunnie should appear often enough to feel present but not cover essential controls. Recommended presence:

- Prominent on Today and Sunnie Home
- Medium presence on feature landing screens
- Small reactions on completion states
- Minimal presence in dense forms, maps, tables, and settings

## 14. Visual QA checklist

Before approving a Sunnie asset:

- Does he look as young as the canonical sheet?
- Is his head large and face round?
- Are the eyes large and warm?
- Is the body compact?
- Does the outfit fit his proportions rather than changing them?
- Does he remain recognizable in silhouette?
- Is the expression kind?
- Does the asset work at intended screen size?
- Is there a reduced-motion/static equivalent if animated?
