# Product Vision and Goals

## Vision statement

Sunnie Days is the happiest useful app Vanessa owns: a beautiful, private companion that helps her manage an irregular travel life, care for her home jungle, stay emotionally grounded, prepare food, record memories, and enjoy intelligent play. Sunnie turns practical routines into gentle, rewarding moments without trivializing them.

## Product promise

When Vanessa opens Sunnie Days, she should immediately understand what matters today and feel supported rather than managed. The app should reduce cognitive load, preserve memories, and make repetitive care tasks feel personal.

## Primary goals

### G-01: Unify daily support

Bring travel, plants, wellness, meals, journal, and games into one coherent app with shared context, not six disconnected mini-apps.

### G-02: Solve real travel problems

Support packing, trip preparation, time zones, weather, sleep, hydration, meals, layovers, return routines, and plant handoff around an irregular flight-attendant schedule.

### G-03: Make 50+ plants manageable

Provide deep records, schedules, bulk actions, search, filters, health history, photos, and travel coverage without making plant care feel clinical.

### G-04: Provide a complete gentle wellness layer

Offer useful check-ins, journaling, meditations, breathing, calm sounds, affirmations, and trends while avoiding diagnosis, pressure, or guilt.

### G-05: Make food planning practical

Help decide, shop, prep, pack, store, and use food around work and travel. Respect the permanent no-eggs rule.

### G-06: Offer original intelligent games

Create distinctive word, language, memory, trivia, and logic experiences tied to travel, plants, Sunnie, and progression. Avoid template clones.

### G-07: Establish Sunnie as a consistent companion

Sunnie’s appearance, voice, tone, outfits, reactions, and home must remain coherent across every feature.

### G-08: Reward without coercion

Use collections, levels, scenes, decor, and positive milestones. Never remove earned rewards or shame missed activity.

### G-09: Integrate with Apple devices naturally

Use Apple Health and Apple Watch where they improve convenience. Keep permissions optional and the app fully useful without them.

### G-10: Remain expandable

New games, themes, destination packs, outfits, music, affirmations, meditations, plant tools, and future companion apps should fit through stable extension points.

## Experience goals

The app should feel:

- Warm
- Whimsical
- Polished
- Calm
- Clever
- Personal
- Adult-cute
- Rich but not cluttered
- Playful without becoming childish

## Anti-goals

Sunnie Days is not:

- A public social network
- A productivity app that scolds the user
- A medical diagnostic product
- A flight operations or safety-critical aviation system
- A generic habit tracker with a mascot pasted on top
- A plant database with no emotional design
- A game arcade unrelated to the rest of the app
- A web app disguised as an iPhone app
- A platform for ads, subscriptions, or paid currency

## Success measures for private use

Because this is initially a personal app, success is measured primarily through observed usefulness rather than growth metrics:

- Vanessa can identify today’s important actions in under 15 seconds.
- Plant-care tasks for a 50+ collection remain manageable.
- Trip preparation requires fewer separate notes and apps.
- She can log a mood or plant action in a few taps.
- The app remains pleasant after months of daily use.
- Sunnie’s behavior never feels judgmental or repetitive.
- New content can be added without destabilizing existing features.
