# Sunnie Days Glossary

**Sunnie** — The young male sloth companion and central character.

**Sunnie Days** — The app name. Also the branded day-cycle presentation for morning/day within the optional day-cycle theme.

**Sunnie Afternoonies** — The branded afternoon presentation.

**Sunnie Nights** — The branded evening/night/late-night presentation.

**Universal time engine** — App-wide system that adapts lighting, color, suggestions, greetings, and audio based on local context. It operates independently of the selected theme.

**Theme** — A data-driven visual and audio skin, such as Lush Tropical Jungle or Travel Scrapbook.

**Jungle** — The plant-care area and Vanessa’s large plant collection.

**Sunnie Home** — The evolving collectible environment containing Sunnie, plants, decor, travel objects, outfits, and music.

**Destination pack** — Downloadable app-authored content for a place. It may include artwork, outfits, stamps, postcards, trivia, music, and guides. It does not mean downloadable offline Apple map tiles.

**Creator audio** — Music or sound added behind the scenes by the product owner. Vanessa does not import or manage source MIDI.

**Care event** — An append-only record of a completed plant action such as watering or fertilizing.

**Domain event** — A typed internal event used to update summaries, progression, rewards, and cross-feature reactions without direct feature coupling.

**Vertical slice** — A complete end-to-end user flow that crosses UI, business logic, persistence, sync, progression, and testing.

**Final 2D release** — The complete native app described here, before future voice, advanced animation, 3D, Android, caretaker, or LifeOS extensions.
