# Persona and Use Context

## Primary user

**Name:** Vanessa  
**Age:** 38  
**Occupation:** Flight attendant  
**Languages:** Fluent Spanish; some Portuguese and French; English assumed  
**Home:** Filled with more than 50 plants, especially tropical plants; she calls it “the jungle”  
**Dietary constraint:** No eggs  
**Interests:** Travel, plants, pretty visual design, sloths, wordplay, trivia, memory, languages, logic, wellness, and collecting

## Relevant personal context

- Travel and work schedules are irregular.
- She may cross time zones and alternate between home, work travel, layovers, and recovery days.
- Plant-care coverage changes depending on trip length. Her mother sometimes helps; plants may also be left alone.
- Meal challenges include deciding what to eat, preparing ahead, eating well while working, and packing meals or snacks.
- Favorite places and destination priorities include Vietnam, Tokyo, Paris, Spain, and Brazil.
- Sunnie began as an affectionate “sleepy sloth” joke and is emotionally meaningful, not a random mascot.
- “Noonies” is a personal nickname that should be used sparingly and naturally.

## Core usage contexts

### Morning or start of day

- Review travel status
- See plant tasks
- Receive an affirmation
- Complete a quick wellness check-in
- Decide what food to prepare or pack
- Start the daily puzzle

### Before a work trip

- Review departure checklist
- Pack uniform, work items, food, and personal items
- Confirm time zones and weather
- Prepare hydration and sleep plan
- Assign or review plant coverage
- Use food that may spoil

### During a layover

- See local and home time
- Use a short reset, breathing, or meditation
- Review meal/snack plan
- Record a memory or place
- Play a short puzzle

### Returning home

- Use return-home checklist
- Review plants needing attention
- Start recovery routine
- Unpack food and update pantry
- Add trip memories and stamps

### Plant-care session

- View due tasks
- Log several plants efficiently
- Add photos or health observations
- Use QR identity later
- See gentle progress and Sunnie reactions

### Quiet evening or night

- Journal
- Review gratitude
- Play calm sounds
- Use meditation or sleep wind-down
- Visit Sunnie’s home
- Browse travel memories or collections

## Design implications

- Important actions must be reachable with one hand.
- The interface must tolerate interruptions and preserve draft state.
- Time-zone handling must be explicit and testable.
- Offline use is essential during travel.
- Quick logging must coexist with deep detail screens.
- The app should never assume a conventional Monday-to-Friday routine.
- Notifications must adapt to travel and quiet hours.
- Dense information should be progressively disclosed rather than placed on one crowded dashboard.
