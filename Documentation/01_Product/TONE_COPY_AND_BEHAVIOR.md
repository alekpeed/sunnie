# Tone, Copy, and Behavior Guide

## Core voice

Sunnie is sweet, happy, caring, gentle, and sincere. He can be sleepy, playful, curious, proud, delighted, or calm. He is never sarcastic, snide, demanding, manipulative, disappointed, cynical, or frightening.

## Writing characteristics

- Short, natural sentences
- Clear actions
- Warm without excessive baby talk
- Occasional playful wording
- No forced enthusiasm on every screen
- No vague wellness clichés when practical guidance is available
- No claims of knowing the user’s emotions beyond what she records

## Approved patterns

### Encouragement

- “One little thing at a time.”
- “I saved this for whenever you’re ready.”
- “Your jungle is looking cared for.”
- “That was a good step.”
- “We can keep today simple.”

### Missed task

- “This is still waiting when you have a moment.”
- “No pressure. We can move it to another day.”
- “This plant may be ready for a little attention.”

### Travel

- “Your bag is almost ready.”
- “Paris is three hours ahead of home.”
- “Let’s make the return home easy.”

### Wellness

- “How are you feeling right now?”
- “Would a two-minute pause help?”
- “You can skip this and come back later.”

## Prohibited patterns

- “You failed.”
- “You broke your streak.”
- “Sunnie is disappointed.”
- “Your plants are dying because you forgot.”
- “You ignored me.”
- “No excuses.”
- “You must do this now.”
- Fake urgency
- Threats of lost rewards
- Negative labels such as lazy, bad, unhealthy, careless, or irresponsible

## Error copy

Errors should identify what happened and what remains safe.

Example:

> “That didn’t sync yet. Your entry is saved on this phone, and I’ll try again when iCloud is available.”

Avoid generic blame such as “You did something wrong.”

## Nickname rule

“Noonies” may appear in approximately 5 percent of eligible Sunnie messages.

Eligible:

- Greetings
- Celebrations
- Casual affirmations
- Postcards
- Friendly home scenes

Ineligible:

- Permission requests
- Errors
- Privacy notices
- Health explanations
- Travel document alerts
- Any urgent or sensitive copy

## Reminder tone

Reminder frequency may adapt, but emotional pressure may not. Increasing regularity means offering another gentle opportunity, not escalating language.

## Localization rule

The emotional tone must survive translation. Do not translate word-for-word when that produces unnatural or infantilizing language. Game localization may use Spanish, Portuguese, and French content, but the main application language can begin with English.
