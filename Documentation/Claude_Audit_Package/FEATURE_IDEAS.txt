FEATURE IDEAS FOR LATER

Prepared on August 7, 2026

Do not start these until the release blockers in AUDIT_REPORT.txt are fixed.

SMALL IDEAS WITH HIGH VALUE

1. A “today is hard” switch.

   For one day, the app could hide scores, reduce reminders, use shorter plans,
   and offer only gentle choices. It should reset by itself the next day and
   never remove anything the user earned.

2. A weekly private postcard.

   Sunnie could make a short, editable summary of plants cared for, meals planned,
   calm minutes, and trip moments. Nothing should leave the phone unless the user
   chooses to share it.

3. A plant handover card.

   Make a simple printable or shareable card for a caretaker. It could show only
   the selected plants, dates, and care steps, without exposing journal, health,
   home, or trip history.

4. A “why am I seeing this?” button.

   Reminders and suggestions could explain their reason in one sentence, such as
   “This plant is two days past its usual watering day.” This builds trust and
   helps the owner notice bad information.

5. A private app lock.

   Offer an optional face or passcode check before opening journal and wellness
   areas. Keep the rest of the app, such as plant care, quick to open.

6. A recovery and backup checkup.

   A settings card could say when the last private device backup succeeded and
   let the owner make a fresh export. It should never pretend a backup exists
   unless the app can truly confirm it.

7. Seasonal plant review.

   At gentle intervals, Sunnie could ask whether light, room, pot, and care timing
   still fit. The result should be a suggestion, never a diagnosis.

8. Accessibility presets.

   Alongside following the phone's settings, offer simple choices like “less
   movement,” “stronger contrast,” “quiet mode,” and “extra-simple screens.”

LARGER IDEAS

1. A shared caretaker mode with limited access.

   A trusted person could see and finish only chosen plant tasks for a set time.
   They should never see journal, wellness, meals, or unrelated travel details.

2. A travel day card for poor connections.

   Put the day's places, packing reminders, plant handover details, and chosen
   emergency notes into one page that works without a connection.

3. A personal pattern viewer.

   Let the owner compare routines without judgments: for example, sleep with calm
   practice, or travel days with meal planning. Always say that a pattern is not
   proof and never turn it into medical advice.

FEATURES TO AVOID FOR NOW

1. Public profiles, leaderboards, or competitive streaks.
2. Advertising or outside tracking.
3. Automatic health conclusions.
4. A talking helper that sends private writing to an outside service by default.
5. More games before the current app is proven stable and accessible.
