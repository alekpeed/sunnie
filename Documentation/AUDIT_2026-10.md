# Code audit — October 2026

Run on Oct 3 2026, after every branch and open pull request was brought into one
line of history. Read alongside `ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md`: several things that
look like gaps are decisions, and they are listed under "Not findings" so they
are not re-raised.

The bar for "checked clean" below is that the property was searched for across
the whole codebase, not sampled. Where only a sample or a heuristic was
possible, the entry says so.

## Open — high

**H1. A deleted journal entry is restorable for thirty days, and the app says so —
but there is no screen to restore it from.** After the "Undo" banner goes, the
entry is unreachable until it is purged on day 31. The message in
`JournalScreens.swift` ("You can bring it back for the next 30 days") is true of
the data and false of the app. `deletedEntries()` has no UI caller. ADR-033 named
this as open work; it is the most important unfinished feature, because it
breaks a promise made at the moment of deleting something personal.

**H2. All artwork is placeholder.** Sunnie is drawn as a simple shape by
`SunnieAvatarView`; the asset catalog contains no images. Every layer in
`AssetsSource/ASSET_MANIFEST.md` is placeholder or not started. The swap is
contained — that one view is the only place Sunnie becomes pixels.

**H3. Nothing has run on a real device.** Health, the QR camera, haptics, audio
interruption, and Watch transfers (TEST-003 requires paired physical devices)
remain unproven. Blocked on the TestFlight setup.

## Open — medium

**M1. The Today front page bypasses localization.** About thirty user-facing
strings are hardcoded in `TodayScreen.swift`, including lines in Sunnie's voice
("You've got this!", "Tell Sunnie anything you want to capture."), which the
project rules say must not be hardcoded in screens. None are in
`Localizable.strings`; the localization validator does not catch strings passed
as `String` rather than `LocalizedStringKey`.

**M2. Export covers the Jungle and the journal only.** Wellness check-ins and
sessions, hydration, trips, packing and travel memories, recipes and meal plans,
collections, and the photo and voice-note files themselves are not exported.
PRIV-002 asks for app-owned data generally.

**M3. Tell Sunnie has no tests.** 553 lines, including speech handling and intent
routing, with the observable model living in the screen file.

**M4. Speech can leave the device.** `TellSunnieScreen.swift` requires on-device
recognition only when the device supports it, and otherwise sends audio to
Apple. iOS's own permission prompt discloses this, so it is not a broken
promise, but it sits awkwardly beside "nothing leaves your phone". A decision for
the owner: keep the fallback, or offer typing instead when on-device is
unavailable.

**M5. Migrations are tested only against empty stores.** Every prior schema is
opened at the current version, but no populated store has been migrated, and
ADR-017's namespace freeze is owed before any change that reshapes a model.

**M6. The first TestFlight build will have no Health, widget data, weather, or
iCloud sync.** Entitlements are commented out in `Config/*.xcconfig` by design
(ADR-012) and the TestFlight workflow does not enable them. Documented in
`TESTFLIGHT_SETUP.md`; turning them on needs Apple Developer portal setup first.

## Open — low

- **L1.** While Today loads, the greeting reads "Good morning" at any hour
  (`TodayScreen.swift`, the fallback in `greetingCard`).
- **L2.** One silent delete remains, against ADR-033's rule:
  `ManageHome.swift` clears a decor slot with `try?`.
- **L3.** `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription` is declared but unused — photos come
  through `PhotosPicker`, which needs no permission — and `CapabilityBroker`
  reports a photo-library status that gates nothing.
- **L4.** Comments that say working things do not work: `CalmSoundsScreen` says
  playback is "a no-op until Phase 10" (sounds play), and `MoreScreen` says
  everything but Themes and Settings "routes to a stub" (every destination now
  opens its real screen). On a project worked by several agents, a stale comment
  is an instruction.
- **L5.** `SettingsScreen` manages its own temporary export directory with
  `FileManager`, a small exception to views not touching the file system.

## Checked clean

- **SwiftData predicates.** All 121 `#Predicate` bodies reviewed for constructs
  that compile but translate differently — `!=` on optional columns, `.isEmpty`,
  `??`, optional chaining, string matching. The three earlier defects of this
  kind are fixed; none remain.
- **Privacy boundary.** The iPhone, Watch, and widget code make no network
  calls; the only URL is WeatherKit's attribution link. No analytics,
  advertising, or crash-reporting SDK, and no third-party Swift package at all.
- **Data at rest.** App-wide data protection in the entitlements, plus explicit
  protection on media and intent-handoff files.
- **Crash-prone code.** No `try!` or `as!`. Force-unwraps are on constant URLs
  or on URLs built from UUIDs. `fatalError` appears only where the schema itself
  is invalid.
- **Product rules.** No "Sunnie Mornings" or "Sunnie Evenings" in shipped content,
  and `ContentValidator` rejects them. No streak, guilt, or "overdue" language in
  user-facing strings. No shipped recipe contains eggs.
- **Watch actions.** Every action carries an idempotency key and travels as a
  queued transfer.
- **Lifecycle.** Expired journal entries are purged at launch and in background
  maintenance; export files are removed after sharing; Siri and Shortcuts
  hand-off falls back to app storage when the App Group is unavailable.
- **Architecture.** Screens do not reach persistence or Apple frameworks
  directly, with the one exception in L5.
- **Accessibility, mechanically.** Every button carries a VoiceOver label, which
  a UI test enforces. This is not a substitute for using the app with VoiceOver
  and large text by hand, which has not been done.

## Not findings

- **No erase-everything control.** ADR-033: deliberate, at the owner's direction.
  Deleting the app is the erase path.
- **iCloud sync off; entitlements inactive.** ADR-012, pending real signing.
- **No composed music.** Declared tracks were never recorded; synthesised sound
  works.
