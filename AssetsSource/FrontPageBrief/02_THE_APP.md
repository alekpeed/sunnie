# What the app actually is

**Read this before generating any screen mockup.** The concept art in
`Documentation/Reference_Images/Canonical/` is beautiful and, on this one point,
out of date: `Sunnie_Canon_03_Wellness_Concept.png` shows a four-tab app called
"a cozy mood journal" with tabs **Home / Journal / Stats / Me**.

That is not what was built. Generating front-page images from the concept would
produce marketing for an app that does not exist — a wrong tab bar is the kind of
detail nobody notices until every image has it.

---

## The real shape

**Sunnie Days.** A native iPhone app: a gentle daily companion built for one
person. Sunnie is her sloth. It is warm, unhurried, and never nags.

**Five tabs**, in this order:

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Today** | The front page. A greeting from Sunnie, the plant care waiting today, a wellness line, an affirmation |
| **Jungle** | Her houseplants — care schedules, history, each plant's own page |
| **Travel** | Trips, destinations, and countdowns |
| **Wellness** | Check-ins, breathing, meditation, and a private journal |
| **More** | Games, meals, collections, Sunnie's Home, themes, settings |

## The Today screen, in the order it actually renders

Use this for the hero mockup — it is the composition of the built screen:

1. **Greeting card** — Sunnie, prominent, with a message in his voice
2. **Plant card** — headed *"Your jungle"*, with the plant name, the care type,
   a status chip (*"Waiting 2 days"* / *"Due today"*), and a primary button
   reading **"Mark watered"**
3. **Wellness section** — a check-in prompt and an affirmation
4. Further cards below

Real copy that appears on it, if you want legible text in a mockup:
*"Your jungle"*, *"Mark watered"*, *"Waiting 2 days"*,
*"2 may be ready for a little attention"*, *"Monstera"*, *"Bird of Paradise"*,
*"Fiddle Leaf Fig"*, *"Pothos"*, *"Calathea"*.

## The three day cycles

The app changes with the time of day, and the names are fixed:

| Time | Name shown |
|---|---|
| Morning and day | **Sunnie Days** |
| Afternoon | **Sunnie Afternoonies** |
| Evening, night, and late night | **Sunnie Nights** |

**Never write "Sunnie Mornings" or "Sunnie Evenings".** They do not exist, and
they are the single easiest mistake to make here. The app is always titled
*Sunnie Days* regardless of which presentation is showing.

A set of three images — one per cycle — is the most natural way to show what the
app *is*, and it is the thing no single screenshot can convey.

## Tone, which shows in pictures as much as in words

- Kind, warm, unhurried. Never sarcastic, guilt-tripping, or urgent.
- **No streak counters, no broken-streak imagery, no "you missed a day".** The
  app deliberately has none of that, and a generated image implying it would be
  advertising the opposite of the product.
- Sunnie is never disappointed in her.
- Nothing medical, nothing diagnostic, no charts implying a health verdict.

## Two content facts worth knowing

- **No eggs.** If you generate any food or meal imagery, there are no eggs in it.
  That is a standing dietary rule in the product, not a stylistic preference.
- **No MIDI, no music-management UI.** Music ingestion is creator-side only and
  is never a screen she sees.

## What is placeholder today

Useful context, because it is why these images are being made at all: **no
production Sunnie artwork exists in the app yet.** In the running build he is a
circle with a symbol in it. These images are the first real depiction, so they
set the standard rather than matching something already there.
