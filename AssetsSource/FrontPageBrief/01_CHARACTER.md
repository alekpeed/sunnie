# Sunnie — the invariant character brief

**Paste this into every image prompt.** It is the part that must not drift.

This file is the *locked* half of the brief. Composition, scene, crop, and props
are free — see `README.md`. What is fixed is who Sunnie is: `CLAUDE.md` states
outright that he must not be redesigned as tall, mature, realistic, lanky, or
adult-proportioned, and those are precisely the directions an image generator
wanders in unprompted.

Everything here is drawn from `Documentation/02_Character_and_Design/SUNNIE_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`
and the canonical sheet at
`Documentation/Reference_Images/Canonical/Sunnie_Canon_01_Character_Sheet.png`.
Attach that PNG to the generator as a style reference wherever the tool allows
it — a picture holds the character better than any paragraph, and the paragraphs
below exist for the tools that will not take one.

## Copy-paste block

> Sunnie is a **young, baby-faced male sloth** drawn as a soft plush toy —
> cute, rounded, and childlike. Soft-textured cream and light-tan fur. A large
> round head with a **broad rounded cream face mask**, soft darker-brown patches
> around the eyes, **large glossy dark-brown eyes with warm highlights**, a small
> dark-brown nose, a small gentle smile, **peach-pink cheek blush**, short soft
> eyebrows, and a **small tuft of fur** on top of his head. Short plush arms and
> legs with small rounded claws. He wears a **butter-yellow knitted onesie with a
> small smiling sun on the chest**, and is often with a **lavender blanket
> patterned with cream stars**.
>
> Proportions: **his head is roughly 42–48% of his standing height and slightly
> wider than his torso.** Body short and compact, limbs short enough that his
> hands stay near his torso. He must read as a small toy or a child's mascot.
>
> Style: **soft watercolour and gouache children's-book illustration.** Gentle
> shading, soft edges, no harsh outlines, warm cream background. Cosy, calm,
> affectionate.

## Never (these are hard rules, not preferences)

The character bible names these as failure states, and every one of them is a
plausible thing a generator will drift toward if not told otherwise:

- **Never tall, mature, realistic, lanky, or adult-proportioned.**
- Never broad-shouldered or adult-human in torso shape.
- Never a long or narrow face — the face is broad and round.
- Never small or naturalistic eyes. Oversized is correct; real sloth anatomy is not.
- Never a redesign. If a pose is not in the canon sheet, keep the face, the
  proportions, and the onesie exactly and change only the pose.
- **Never a negative expression.** Sleepy, calm, and quiet are all fine. Sad,
  cross, disappointed, scolding, or smug are not — the app never implies Sunnie
  is let down by the person using it.

## Expressions that exist in the canon

Use these names; they match the reference sheet:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sleepy** | Eyes closed, resting, nightcap, curled up |
| **Happy** | Sitting upright, open smile, holding a small sun |
| **Wave** | Standing, one arm raised, bright and greeting |
| **Hugs pillow** | Eyes closed, holding a lavender star cushion |
| **Warm sip** | Half-closed eyes, both hands round a mug with a heart |
| **Stretch** | Arms up, eyes closed, blanket behind |

## The props that belong to his world

Drawn from the canon sheet, and worth naming so the generator furnishes scenes
consistently: a crescent moon with a sleeping face, soft clouds, small stars in
butter-yellow and lavender, small pink hearts, sprigs of sage-green leaves, a
small potted plant in a cream pot, a mug with a heart on it, a short stack of
pastel books, and a smiling star plushie.

His traits, from the sheet: loves cosy naps; favourite time is golden hour;
brings warmth and calm; always with a cosy drink; comfort items are pillows and
blankets.

## Checklist before accepting an image

Straight from the bible's visual QA list:

- Does he look as young as the canonical sheet?
- Is his head large and his face round?
- Are the eyes large and warm?
- Is the body compact?
- Does the outfit fit his proportions rather than changing them?
- Is he recognisable in silhouette?
- Is the expression kind?
